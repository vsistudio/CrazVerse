Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

What we collect

When you join CrazVerse, we store the player profile details you provide: username, age, gender selection, and basic Google account identity details used to connect your account.

How it is used

This data creates and protects your player identity. CrazVerse does not request or store your Google password.

Data protection

Player data is stored in Cloud Firestore and access is restricted to the signed-in player by the deployed security rules.
